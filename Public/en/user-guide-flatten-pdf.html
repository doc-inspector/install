PDF Flattening Guide

\n

Lock your forms and interactive layers into static images.

\n
\n
❌ The Problem
\n

Interactive PDFs (forms with checkboxes, drop-downs, layers) can be altered by anyone even after you have filled them out and signed them.

\n
\n
\n
✅ The Solution
\n

Flattening rasterizes the entire page into a high-quality, high-DPI image, permanently fusing the form data to the page so it can no longer be edited.

\n
\n
\n
⚙️ How it Works
\n
    \n
  • Upload your filled-out interactive PDF.
    • \n
  • The tool renders every page into a high-resolution image.
    • \n
  • The images are repackaged back into a standard PDF format.
    • \n
  • Download the flat, static document.
    • \n
