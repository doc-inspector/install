Why Every Law Firm Needs a Document Inspection Tool

Legal professionals handle some of the most sensitive documents in existence — court filings, witness statements, financial records, medical reports. Every document that leaves your firm carries your reputation with it.

The metadata problem in legal work

Opposing counsel can (and does) examine document metadata. A carelessly shared Word file might reveal tracked changes showing your negotiation strategy, internal comments between partners, or the original filename that hints at case strategy.

Evidence integrity

When building evidence bundles for court, you need absolute certainty that documents haven't been tampered with and don't contain hidden content. A PDF with embedded JavaScript, for instance, could be flagged as potentially compromised evidence.

The DocInspector workflow for legal teams

  1. Audit — scan incoming document packages, identify risks and metadata
  2. Report — generate Excel reports documenting exactly what was found
  3. Repair & Harden — rebuild PDFs, strip metadata, flatten to image-only if needed
  4. Bundle — create clean, court-ready document packages with preserved structure

All processing happens on your firm's machines. No documents touch the cloud. Full GDPR and attorney-client privilege compliance by design.
