What Is an Air-Gapped Computer? Security Explained

2026-06-30  ·  By DocInspector Team

An air-gapped computer is a machine physically isolated from the internet and all local networks. No Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth, no Ethernet — nothing in or out except physical media. Governments, banks, and intelligence agencies use them to protect their most sensitive data.

Why Air Gaps Exist

Every network connection is a potential attack vector. Malware, remote exploits, man-in-the-middle attacks — they all require connectivity. Remove the connection, and you eliminate the entire class of network-based threats.

Air gaps are used for: nuclear facility controls, classified government systems, financial transaction signing, and long-term secret storage.

Practical Use: Documents & PDFs

Most organizations don't need a full air-gapped workstation, but they do need air-gapped document practices: cleaning metadata before sharing, encrypting files before transit, and redacting sensitive data before distribution.

Air Gap vs. Encryption: Which Do You Need?

Air gaps prevent remote access. Encryption protects data in transit and at rest. They solve different problems — serious security programs use both.

  • Air gap: stops network intrusion
  • Encryption: protects the file even if stolen

Limits of Air Gaps

Air gaps aren't invincible. The famous Stuxnet worm spread via USB drives into air-gapped Iranian nuclear facilities. Physical access, compromised hardware, and insider threats still apply.

FAQ

Can a laptop be air-gapped?
Yes — disable all wireless hardware, disable Bluetooth in BIOS, and never connect it to a network. Use USB only with scanned, verified media.
Is air-gapping necessary for PDFs?
Rarely. For most use cases, combining encryption, redaction, and metadata removal gives strong protection without the operational cost of air-gapping.
What operating system is best for an air-gapped PC?
Tails OS or a hardened Linux installation with disabled network modules are common choices. Windows can work if network hardware is physically removed.
