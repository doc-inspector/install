NIS2 Directive: Document Security Compliance Guide 2025

2026-06-30  ·  By DocInspector Team

The EU's NIS2 Directive (Network and Information Security) came into force in January 2023 with member states required to transpose it into national law by October 2024. If your organization operates in the EU or processes EU data, NIS2 applies to you — and document security is a core compliance area.

What NIS2 Requires for Documents

NIS2 doesn't prescribe specific technical controls, but requires appropriate and proportionate technical measures to manage cybersecurity risks. For document workflows, this translates to:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Access controls — who can read, edit, share documents
  • Audit trails — logging who accessed what and when
  • Incident response — ability to detect and contain document breaches
  • Supply chain security — controlling third-party document access

Which Organizations Are Covered

NIS2 covers essential entities (energy, transport, banking, healthcare, water, digital infrastructure) and important entities (postal services, waste management, chemical manufacturing, food, digital providers). SMEs in these sectors are included if they exceed 50 employees or €10M annual turnover.

Document Encryption as a NIS2 Control

Encrypting PDFs containing sensitive business data is a straightforward, auditable NIS2 technical measure. Password protection with strong encryption (AES-256) satisfies the "encryption in transit and at rest" requirement for document assets.

FAQ

Does NIS2 require PDF encryption specifically?
NIS2 doesn't mandate specific technologies. It requires appropriate technical measures. PDF encryption with AES-256 is a recognized best practice that satisfies this requirement for document assets.
What are the fines for NIS2 non-compliance?
Essential entities face fines up to €10M or 2% of global annual turnover. Important entities face up to €7M or 1.4% of global turnover.
How does NIS2 differ from GDPR for document security?
GDPR focuses on personal data. NIS2 covers all critical operational data and security of the systems that process it. They overlap but NIS2 has broader organizational scope.
