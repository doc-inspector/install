Overview

In high-stakes Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A) due diligence, sellers must present extensive financial records, legal contracts, IP portfolios, and operational data to prospective buyers, investment bankers, legal counsels, and cleanroom advisors. However, sharing un-sanitized raw documents exposes companies to severe legal liabilities, loss of trade secrets, competitor intelligence leaks, and catastrophic GDPR compliance fines under Article 83 (up to €20 million or 4% of annual global turnover).

🛡️ Why GDPR & Security Compliance Matter in M&A Cleanrooms

Exposing un-redacted employee PII, customer banking details, or hidden author metadata (such as internal comments, software fingerprints, and GPS coordinates from embedded camera photos) during due diligence violates international privacy frameworks.
