PDF Text Redaction Guide

\n

Permanently destroy sensitive text data.

\n
\n
❌ The Problem
\n

Simply drawing a black box over text in a standard PDF editor does not delete the text. Anyone can highlight, copy, and paste the text out from under the black box.

\n
\n
\n
✅ The Solution
\n

Our redaction tool finds the exact words, removes the underlying text characters from the PDF source code, replaces them with solid black boxes, and flattens the file.

\n
\n
\n
⚙️ How it Works
\n
    \n
  • Upload your PDF and input the keywords you wish to redact.
    • \n
  • The system locates the bounding boxes of every text match.
    • \n
  • The underlying characters are deleted and replaced with a black vector shape.
    • \n
  • Download the permanently redacted file.
    • \n
