PDF/A Archival Guide (ISO 19005)

\n

The gold standard for 50+ year electronic preservation.

\n
\n
❌ The Problem
\n

Government portals, electronic courts (like e-Dosar), and notaries actively reject standard PDFs because they can break in the future if fonts go missing.

\n
\n
\n
✅ The Solution
\n

The PDF/A conversion process completely locks the document. It forcibly embeds all fonts, removes active scripts, and normalizes colors, guaranteeing future readability.

\n
\n
\n
⚙️ How it Works
\n
    \n
  • Drag a folder of mixed documents (Word, Excel, Images, standard PDFs) into the system.
    • \n
  • The engine applies intelligent Fit-to-Page pre-conversions to Office documents.
    • \n
  • Ghostscript rebuilds the files to strict ISO 19005 PDF/A-2b standards, embedding all necessary fonts.
    • \n
  • Download the validated ZIP, keeping your original folder structure perfectly intact.
