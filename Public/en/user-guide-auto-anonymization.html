Smart Auto-Anonymization (GDPR)

AI Pattern Recognition Zero Data Leak OCR-Powered Redaction
The Problem

Publishing public records, legal verdicts, or business dossiers while complying with GDPR and privacy laws requires censoring personal data. Manually searching for every identification number, IBAN, bank card, and phone number across thousands of scanned pages is error-prone, leaving severe regulatory liability.

The Solution

Automatically scans and permanently blacks-out sensitive personal identifiers across entire directory trees. Recognizes universal patterns (Emails, Phones, Credit Cards, IBANs, IP addresses) and regional national IDs (Moldova IDNO, Romania CNP, US SSN). Deeply connected to the Rebuild & OCR engine to detect text even inside tilted or low-resolution scans. Flattened output guarantees irreversible deletion.

How It Works — Step by Step
  • 1Select which PII categories to redact: National IDs (CNP/IDNO), IBAN/Bank accounts, Credit Cards, Phone numbers, Emails, or Custom Regex.
  • 2Enable Smart OCR to process scanned paper documents and photographs alongside digital PDFs.
  • 3Configure redaction styling: solid black privacy bars or anonymization replacement tokens (e.g. [REDACTED_CNP]).
  • 4Select the source directory containing confidential records.
  • 5Process the collection — all sensitive terms are destroyed irreversibly and vector layers flattened.
